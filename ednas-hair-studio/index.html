About the studio

Traditional barbering. Modern grooming.

Ednas Hair Studio is a men's hair salon and grooming studio in Thazhe Chovva, Kannur. We opened on 21 October 2026 with a simple idea: bring the craft of traditional barbering together with the comfort and care of modern grooming.

From a sharp haircut and a well-defined beard to a relaxing head massage or facial, everything happens in one clean, welcoming space, so you can walk in, sit back and walk out feeling like yourself, only better.
